# Privacy Policy for WallpaperX

**Effective Date:** September 22, 2026

WallpaperX ("we", "our", or "the app") is a wallpaper application that allows users to browse, search, view, download, save, and share wallpapers.

## Information We Collect

WallpaperX does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect names, email addresses, passwords, or payment information.

The app may access or use device capabilities when necessary to provide its features, including:

- Internet/network access to retrieve wallpaper content.
- Photos/media or storage access when required to save wallpapers.
- Locally stored app data such as favorite wallpapers, recently viewed wallpapers, and app settings.
- Device sharing functionality when a user chooses to share a wallpaper.

## Third-Party Services

WallpaperX retrieves wallpaper content through third-party services, including the Pexels API.

Third-party services may process technical information such as network requests and IP addresses according to their own privacy policies.

## How We Use Information

Information and device capabilities are used to:

- Display and search wallpapers.
- Download wallpapers to the device.
- Save favorite wallpapers.
- Maintain recently viewed wallpapers.
- Share wallpapers through the device sharing functionality.
- Maintain basic app preferences.

## Data Storage

Favorites, recently viewed wallpapers, and app settings may be stored locally on the user's device.

WallpaperX does not operate its own user account database for these features.

## Data Sharing

WallpaperX does not sell users' personal information.

When users choose to use a third-party service, such as sharing content through another application or retrieving content from a third-party service, information may be processed by that third party according to its own privacy policy.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app data remains on the user's device until it is removed by the user, cleared through the device's app settings, or removed when the app is uninstalled.

Because WallpaperX does not require a user account, there is no separate WallpaperX account containing personal information that users need to request for deletion.

## Children's Privacy

WallpaperX is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns regarding WallpaperX, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the WallpaperX Google Play listing.
